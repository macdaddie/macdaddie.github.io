= The Adventure of the Engineer's Thumb

== Story Guide

The original story runs approximately 6,000 words. For a 36-page picture book
(15 double-page spreads after front matter), target text is ~600 words,
roughly 40–50 words per spread.

This guide lists every spread with its scene, text, and the cats' involvement.

---

=== Page Credits

- Pages 1–3: Front matter (half-title, title, copyright)
- Pages 4–39: Story content (18 spreads, including spread 2.5)
- Page 40: Back matter / colophon

== Spread 1 — The Clock Strikes Seven

*Page 4 — Full-page illustration*

image:scene-01-watsons-consulting-room.png[]

*Scene:* Dr. Watson's consulting room, early morning. A young man sits with a
blood-soaked handkerchief wrapped around his hand. Watson, still in his
dressing-gown, examines him with concern.

*Narration:*

[quote]
____
It was just before seven o'clock when the maid knocked. A young gentleman
had arrived from Paddington Station with a hand wrapped in bloody linen.
His card read: _Mr. Victor Hatherley, Hydraulic Engineer._
____

*Cat note (invisible to text):* On the rooftops visible through the window,
two silhouettes — Catticus and Lollipop — watch the cab that brought
Hatherley. They had tracked him from Paddington.

*Mouse Note (Invisible to text):* Haltherley's mouth is sitting on the table, offering up the thumb, partially exposed and wrapped in a scrap of red stained handkerchief, to Dr. Watson with both paws.

== Spread 2 — A Strange Case

*Pages 6–7 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-02-thumb-theft.png[]

*Scene:* Consulting room. Hatherley sits with his bandaged hand extended.
Watson is half-rising from his chair, one hand reaching out, his face caught
between professional concern and frustrated alarm.

Catticus has jumped on the table and is sniffing the enginner's injured hand. His nose as turned into brass, indicating that those steamborg implants have activated.
At the
doorway, Lollipop has the severed thumb in her mouth, a swift blur dashing
from the room as Watson's shout forms on his lips.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"A cleaver," said he, when I asked. "And not an accident — murderously
intended."

He had brought the thumb with him, wrapped in linen. I stared at it, then
at the wound, then back again. Before I could speak, one of Mrs. Hudson's
cats — the calico — had seized it and was disappearing through the door.

I gave chase, but too late. The cat was gone.

"You must tell this to my friend, Mr. Sherlock Holmes," I said at last,
"before you go to the police."
____

*Cat note:* Lollipop's theft is not random — the severed thumb carries trace
metal residues from the press. She and Catticus will analyse it in their
quarters beneath the kitchen floorboards. Catticus's apparent curiosity at the
wound is equally calculated: his enhanced olfactory sensors are cataloguing
hydraulic oil, nickel dust, and fuller's-earth (the colonel's cover story) —
evidence that the wound is not what it seems.

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock, Hatherley's mouse, stands at the table, on the edge of the handkerchief the thumb was wrapped in, looking bemused at lollopop and waving at her in a friendly fashion.

== Spread 2.5 — Tom's Thumbs

*Pages 8–9 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-025-thumb-fitting.png[]

*Scene:* We are still in Dr. Watson's consulting room at baker street.
The open box of "Tom's Thumbs" sits on the seat, spilling a bizarre assortment of thumb
prosthetics — lady's thumbs with engraved brass nails, worker's thumbs with
can-openers and wrenches, standard thumbs of every size proportional to that of a hand. There are no oversized thumbs.

Watson leans forward, surgical screwdriver in hand, fitting a standard brass
thumb onto Hatherley's right hand where the severed thumb was. Tick-Tock, the
hydro-clockwork mouse, sits on Hatherley's wrist, both forepaws holding the prosthetic
steady in place. Catticus Finch is on the table, nose close to the action, like a curious cat would. his nose is still in it's steamborg transformed state, esentially a normal cat nose but now ornately brass and copper , and a thin blue arc of electrical energy sparks from his nose to
the newly fitted thumb, which glows faintly with electric life.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"We're never going to see that old thumb again; blooming cats!" muttered Dr.
Watson, reaching for a box of "Tom's Thumbs."

"Not to worry, old boy — we'll soon have you fixed up, right as rain," Dr.
Watson said cheerily to Mr. Hatherley, and then to himself as he dropped his
surgical screwdriver: "Blast it, I'm all thumbs today!"
____

*Cat note:* Catticus has followed the cab from Watson's consulting room. His steamborg nose is still manifest from the analysis earlier. The blue spark
is subtle but significant — Catticus can somehow energise brass prosthetics,
imbuing them with a spark of life. The thumb now glows faintly, as though
connected to Hatherley's nervous system.

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock is being unexpectedly helpful, holding the thumb steady
with both forepaws. He seems pleased to have a practical task. He chitters
encouragingly at Watson.

*Prop note:* The Tom's Thumbs box contains a wild assortment. Watson had to
rummage through to find a standard thumb without attachments. A lady's thumb
with an elaborately engraved brass floral nail pattern is visible at the edge
of the box. A larger worker's thumb with a small monkey wrench attachment on the
tip lies beside it. The box lid is branded with a wood-burned logo.


== Spread 3 — Breakfast at Baker Street

*Pages 10–11 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-03-221b-breakfast.png[]

*Scene:* 221B Baker Street. Morning light through the bay window. Holmes in his
dressing-gown, pipe in hand, rashers and eggs on the table. Watson and Hatherley
enter; Hatherley is talking animatedly, his right hand now fitted with a polished
brass steampunk thumb prosthetic, clearly visible as he gestures. On the
mantelpiece, Catticus Finch sits motionless, his brass monocle
has transformed into a short telescope, catching the light. Lollipop curls on
the window seat.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
Holmes received us in his quietly genial fashion, ordered fresh rashers and
eggs, and joined us in a hearty meal. When it was concluded, he settled our
new acquaintance upon the sofa.

"Tell us what you can," said he.
____

*Cat note:* Catticus's monocled eye transforms, becoming slightly telescopic — magnifying,
analysing. Lollipops ears transform into parabolic reflectors, swivel toward Hatherley's voice, cataloguing every
vocal tremor. Neither cat appears to be doing anything but being cats.

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock sits on Hatherley's shoulder giving a hesitant but friendly wave at the cat's with one paw. He's hoping these Steamborg Cats will be his friends.

*Scene:* Flashback — Hatherley's modest Victoria Street office. A gaunt,

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"I am Colonel Lysander Stark," said he. "I need a hydraulic engineer for a
single night's work. The pay is fifty guineas. You will tell no one."

Hungry for work, Hatherley agreed.
____

*Cat note (metaphorical):* No cats in this flashback

*Mouse Note:* you can see three clockwork mice, one sitting on a shelf next to an old oil can that is dripping oil onto a grateful mouse, and another is running along the skirting board towards a mouse hole that has a small but ornate archway over it.

Tick-Tock is in the process of being assembled on Hatherley's desk as Hatherley uses tools to make the final adjustments to his hydraulics.

At eleven o'clock that night, he took the last train from Paddington. At

== Spread 13 — Six Miles Out and Six Miles Back

*Pages 30–31 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-13-map-deduction.png[]

*Scene:* Back at 221B Baker Street. The ordnance map is spread on the
table in Holmes' sitting room. Holmes, Hatherley, Watson, and Bradstreet
argue over the compass directions. Holmes calmly places his finger in
the centre of the circle Bradstreet has drawn.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"I think I could lay my finger on it," said Holmes quietly.

"Really, now!" cried the inspector. "You have formed your opinion! Come,
now, we shall see who agrees with you. I say it is south, for the
country is more deserted there."

"And I say east," said my patient.

"And I am for north," said I.

"You are all wrong."

"But we can't all be."

"Oh, yes, you can. This is my point." He placed his finger in the centre
of the circle. "This is where we shall find them."

"But the twelve-mile drive?" gasped Hatherley.

"Six out and six back. Nothing simpler. You say yourself that the horse
was fresh and glossy when you got in. How could it be if it had gone
twelve miles over heavy roads?"
____

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock frantically waves the notes he took in the
original carriage ride. His heightened steamborg senses recorded the
circuitous route — out six miles, back six miles, in a circle — but
none of the humans pay him any attention.

*Cat note:* The cats notice Tick-Tock's gestures immediately. Lollipop
pins him down with one paw that has transformed into a steampunk paw —
long copper claws, fizzing with electricity. Catticus's monocled eye
becomes more elaborate and projects a cone-shaped green laser pattern of Tick-Tock's
drawing as an overlay to the map, so that Holmes can clearly see that
the carriage drove a circuitous route out of Eyford and then back into
it, to the house which was barely a quarter of a mile from the station.

== Spread 15 — The Coiners Exposed

*Pages 34–35 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-15-coiners-exposed-v4.png[]

*Scene:* The train rattles through the Berkshire countryside. Inside the
compartment, Holmes stands addressing the room. Hatherley, Watson,
and Bradstreet are seated or leaning forward, all eyes on the
compartment wall where a faintly glowing Victorian infographic is projected by Catticus Finch's diffracted monocle cone from
beneath the seat.

The infographic is rendered in delicate green laser light, etched in a
quaintly Victorian style: a diagram showing the coining process from raw
metal blanks through the hydraulic press to finished counterfeit
half-crowns. The style is technical illustration — engraving lines,
labelled components, cross-sections — as though drawn by a Victorian
engineer, but luminous and green, projected on the wooden compartment wall.

Catticus is barely visible beneath the seat, his body hidden in shadow.
system fanning a diffracted cone of green light upward onto the wall. The
Only his head, top hat and brass monocle array is clearly seen — the multi-lens projection
humans see the diagram but not the cat.

Lollipop, curls her way between Holmes' legs in classic cat fashion, extending her segmented brass
scorpion tail upward. The stinger tip traces the path of the process
through the diagram — lingering on the press mechanism and the finished
coins — like a teacher guiding her audience's attention to the right part
of the infographic.

Tick-Tock sits on Hatherley's shoulder, clapping, delightedly, leaning forward with wide eyes, fascinated by the glowing
diagram. The humans stare at the wall, captivated — none of them aware
that the projection is coming from beneath the seat.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"None at all," said Holmes. "They are coiners on a large scale, and have
used the machine to form the amalgam which has taken the place of silver."

"We have known for some time that a clever gang was at work," said the
inspector. "They have been turning out half-crowns by the thousand."

Holmes gestured toward the wall, where a luminous diagram traced the
path from blank metal to counterfeit coin. None of us thought to ask how
it had got there.
____

*Cat note:* Catticus's projection system is the same multi-lens monocle
array used for the map deduction. The diffracted cone of green light is
broader than a single beam — it illuminates a section of the compartment
wall like a soft spotlight. The infographic style is deliberately
antiquated: engraving-line cross-sections, hand-lettered labels, Victorian
technical illustration rendered in green laser light. Lollipop's scorpion
tail is in full extension from the luggage rack, the brass segments
telescoped, the stinger used precisely as a pointer. 

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock is perched on Hatherley's shoulder, leaning
forward,clapping with innocent delight, staring at the
infographic with wide, fascinated eyes. He chitters softly at the
diagram.

== Spread 16 — Smoke Above Eyford

*Pages 36–37 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-16-eyford-smoke.png[]

*Scene:* Eyford station platform. The train has just arrived. The party
steps onto the platform — and freezes. A gigantic column of smoke rises
above the trees beyond the village, hanging like an immense ostrich
feather over the landscape. The house is burning.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
As we rolled into Eyford Station we saw a gigantic column of smoke which
streamed up from behind a small clump of trees in the neighbourhood and
hung like an immense ostrich feather over the landscape.

"A house on fire?" asked Bradstreet as the train steamed off again on
its way.
____

*Cat note:* Just above the men, on the roof of the Eyford station house, Catticus and Lollopop watch the smoke. 
Catticus monocled eye has one again transformed into an ornate, brass telescope which he uses to survey the burning house, from a distance.
Lollopops ears are again transformed into parabolic reflectors as she trains them in the direction of the house to hear anything that may inform the situation further.

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock peers out of Hatherley's pocket at the smoke,
ears flat, his mouth a silent, "O" of alarms and his tiny paws on each cheek.

== Spread 17 — The Smouldering Ruins

*Pages 38–39 — Double-page spread*

image:scene-17-smouldering-ruins-v5.png[]

*Scene:* The party has driven from the station to the site of the house.
What remains is a blackened shell — smouldering timbers, a few licks of flame, collapsed
walls, a chimney stack standing alone against a grey sky. The criminals
have fled. The evidence is ash.

Holmes stands at the edge of the ruins, hands behind his back, studying
the remains. Hatherley stares at the place where he nearly died.
Bradstreet surveys the destruction with professional frustration.
Watson stands beside Hatherley, one hand on his shoulder.

*Narration:*
[quote]
____
"You have had your revenge," said Holmes.

But the criminals were gone.

"I have lost my thumb and my fee. What have I gained?" asked Hatherley.

"Experience," said Holmes.
____

*Cat note:* Catticus is amongst the ruins. His steampumk brass nose as returned and its heightened olfactory senses have lead him to a small pile of half melted, counterfeit coins. He sniffs at them tentatively. His top hat is on his head and his monocle glints, in his right eye.

Lollipop's ears have returned to normal however, the end of her scorpion tail has transformed into the cone of a fire-extinguisher from the end of which a jet of CO2 extinguishes one of isolated fires still burning amongst the wreckage.

*Mouse Note:* Tick-Tock, has returned in Hatherley's pocket but has donned a fireman's hat.
